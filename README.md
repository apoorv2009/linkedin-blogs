# AI System Design: From Real-World Problems to Production-Ready Solutions

A daily LinkedIn series by Apoorv Jain (.NET & AI Architect). Each article starts from a business problem, shows why the obvious fix fails, explains the concept through an everyday analogy, and builds the architecture up one step at a time.

- **Plan, gap analysis, roadmap and visual identity:** [SERIES-PLAN.md](SERIES-PLAN.md)
- **Running example:** Contoso, a fictional company. Every scenario is illustrative unless it is marked as the author's own project.
