# AI System Design: From Real-World Problems to Production-Ready Solutions

A daily LinkedIn series by Apoorv Jain (.NET & AI Architect). Each post starts from a business problem, explains it with an everyday comparison, and builds the design up one step at a time. Every scenario takes place at Contoso, a fictional company, and is illustrative unless it is marked as the author's own project.

- **Plan, roadmap and style guide:** [SERIES-PLAN.md](SERIES-PLAN.md)
- **Articles and cover images:** [articles/](articles/)
- **Length:** a curtain raiser (Part 0), then 31 posts in 7 arcs, one post per weekday.

## Before publishing each post

1. Check the facts against the references at the end of the article.
2. Confirm any first-person project story (ShopSense) matches what you built.
3. Upload the cover image from `articles/images/` (1200 × 627 PNG) with the post.
4. Update the status and add the LinkedIn link in the index above.
